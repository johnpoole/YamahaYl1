# Work log

1966 Yamaha YL1 Twin Jet 100, engine L1-47603, frame Y33-47603. One entry per step, in the order
of the work. The photos are listed with their captions in [Photo_Index.md](Photo_Index.md).

## Strip-down

### 1. Photograph the bike as found

The bike came with no fuel tank, no seat and no side covers. The engine, both carbs, the exhaust,
both wheels, the forks, the handlebars and the headlight were on it. The pressed-steel frame was
bare along the top, the paint worn through and rust showing. With no side covers, the Autolube oil
tank showed on the left side.

Left as found.

### 2. Take out the headlight and speedometer

The headlight rim came off, and the wiring inside the shell was photographed before it was
disturbed. The speedometer came out with its wiring.

Left with the headlight shell empty and the speedometer out.

### 3. Take off the magneto cover

With the cover off, both sets of points, the condenser, the flywheel and the kickstart were open to
view.

Left with the magneto open.

### 4. Take off the carburetors

Both carbs came off. On the bench, the float bowl was full of varnish and dirt.

Left with the carbs on the bench and the manifolds open.

### 5. Put the bike on a work stand

The bike went up on a wooden work stand, wheels clear. The handlebar clamps, the fork tops, the rear
wheel, the chain, the rear brake and the shocks were photographed on the stand.

Left on the stand.

### 6. Take the engine out

The engine was lowered out of the frame with the oil tank resting on it, and set on the floor under
the stand. The exhaust came out with it.

Left with the frame and rear wheel on the stand and the engine on the floor.

### 7. Take off the heads and cylinders

On the bench, the cylinder block and heads came off the cases. The pistons stayed on their rods, and
the engine number L1-47603 is on the case beside them.

Left with the top end off and the pistons on the cases.

### 8. Look inside the frame

Rust inside the pressed-steel frame, with the harness running through it.

Left for the frame inspection.

### 9. Read the frame number

The frame number, Y33-47603, is stamped on the front of the steering head between the fork legs. Its
number part matches the engine's.

State at the end of the strip-down: the frame on the stand with the forks, both wheels, the swingarm,
the shocks and the harness still on it. The engine on the bench with the top end off. The carbs,
headlight and speedometer off.
